Kinh Phật is a Buddhist sutra listening app. We respect your privacy and keep the product focused on calm practice — playlists, search, and playback — without collecting more than the features need.

1. What Kinh Phật is

Kinh Phật lets you listen to Buddhist sutras, search tracks, manage a personal playlist, reorder playback, and use repeat or shuffle. It shows track details such as title, album, and artwork, and may notify you when a track finishes.

2. Data stored on your device

Playlist choices, playback preferences (such as repeat or shuffle), and related local settings are intended to stay on your device for your own listening use. We do not require a cloud account to use the core listening features.

3. No required login for core use

We do not require your name, email, or phone number solely to listen to sutras or manage a local playlist.

4. Permissions

The app only requests permissions needed for features you use, for example:

  • Storage / media access for audio files or offline content you choose to use
  • Notifications so you can receive playback-related alerts (such as when a track finishes)
  • Internet access to load content or show ads when those features are enabled

We do not access unrelated personal files on your device.

5. Advertising

The app may show ads so it can remain free. We do not sell your playlist contents or listening history as a personal data product. Ads, if shown, may be provided by Google AdMob and are not based on a spiritual or practice profile that we create about you.

6. Analytics & crash reports

If enabled, anonymous or aggregated diagnostics (such as crash reports) may help us improve stability. These tools are not used to sell your identity or practice habits.

7. Children

Kinh Phật is a general-audience listening app. It is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest version.

9. Contact / requests

If you have privacy questions, contact us by email or through the support channel on the Google Play or App Store listing where you installed Kinh Phật. We will review promptly.

In short: Kinh Phật is built for peaceful sutra listening. Your playlist and playback preferences are meant to stay on your device. We do not sell your personal listening data. Ads, if shown, help keep the app free.
