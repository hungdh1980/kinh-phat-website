About Kinh Phật

Kinh Phật helps you listen to familiar Buddhist sutras with a clear playlist, gentle visuals, and controls that stay out of the way. It is designed for daily practice — search quickly, build your own queue, and listen with repeat or shuffle.

The app includes well-known works such as the Amitabha Sutra, Ksitigarbha Sutra, Avalokitesvara Sutra, and the Six Roots Repentance ceremony, presented with soft colors that feel quiet and respectful.

Key features

1. Rich sutra library

Listen to familiar Buddhist sutras gathered in one place, with title, album, and artwork shown for each track.

2. Fast search

Find the sutra you want quickly so practice can begin without friction.

3. Personal playlist

Add or remove tracks, then reorder the queue so your listening session matches your practice.

4. Repeat & shuffle

Choose repeat or random playback when you want a longer, uninterrupted listening session.

5. Playback notifications

Get a clear notice when a track finishes, so you always know where you are in the playlist.
